## WHAT
Review of Goldfish 8.0.10 (main @ 0f74184) for the toolkit streamline effort. No source files changed. Measured on the real `~/.goldfish/registry.json` and real `.memories/` data.

## WHY
The 2026-11-03 review (F8) kept cross-project duplicates as a documented tradeoff. New measurements show the cost is much larger than assumed.

## HOW (evidence)
- Registry: 195 entries, 155 point to deleted folders, 6 have no `.memories/`, 34 live. 166 entries are worktrees. Nothing prunes it (`unregisterProject` has no production caller).
- Duplicates: 34 live projects hold 16,097 checkpoint files but 6,452 unique IDs. 9,645 files are copies from worktrees and clones.
- `recall({workspace:"all", search})`: about 15 s on every call (target < 500 ms). `searchCheckpoints` builds a new Orama index over 16,608 documents (23 MB of text) each call; the cross-project path passes no cache key. Deduped corpus: 5.8 s. Deduped plus cached index: 73-96 ms. Results also repeat (10 returned, 8 unique).
- `recall({workspace:"all"})` without dates: 2.4 s cold, 80 ms warm. Date-window and single-project recalls are 2-160 ms.
- Session start: hook 4,619 chars, server instructions 1,998 chars (repeated verbatim inside the hook), tools/list 13,869 chars. The workspace rule appears 6 times (722 chars in each of 3 schemas).
- Razorback writes `.memories/autonomous-run-*.md` at the root of `.memories/`; 124 such files exist across projects and recall cannot see them. Razorback SDD lines 130-134 and cross-model-convergence line 83 still ask for phase and per-round checkpoints.
- Claude plugin cache for goldfish is 689 MB because the marketplace source is the local folder (copies `.worktrees`, `.miller`, `.code-kb`).

## IMPACT
Dedup by checkpoint ID plus a cached cross-project index, and registry pruning, are the top candidates. Both reverse recorded decisions, so they need the owner's approval.
